# User Guide

Log in at the server's address (given to you by your Manager) with your
Employee ID and password. You'll be asked to change your password on first
login.

## Manager

You see everything, every department's screens, and additionally:
- **Users:** create accounts for new employees, assign their department,
  deactivate accounts that should no longer have access.
- **Audit Log:** a complete, unchangeable history of every significant
  action taken in the system - who did what, and when.
- **License & Devices:** see this office's license status and which
  computers are authorized; revoke a device if a computer is decommissioned
  or a license needs to move.
- Only you (not Sub-Admin) can deactivate a user account or revoke a
  device.

## Subordinate Admin

Same access as Manager to day-to-day department screens (Materials,
Requests, Purchasing, Production, Dispatch, Accounts, Costing, Users), but
cannot deactivate user accounts, revoke devices, or create another Manager
account - those stay Manager-only.

## Store

- **Materials & Inventory:** create new materials, and record every stock
  movement (Receive, Issue, Adjustment, Damage, Return) through the "Stock
  Movement" button - never edit a stock number directly, every change is
  logged.
- **Material Requests:** review requests from other departments, Approve or
  Reject, and Fulfill approved requests (this actually issues real stock).
- **Suppliers & Purchase Orders:** manage supplier contacts, create
  purchase orders, and record goods receipts when materials arrive.

## Planning

- **Production Jobs:** create a job with client, work name, pages, copies,
  and priority. Use **Check Availability** to see a GREEN/RED breakdown of
  whether enough of each required material is in stock.
- **Material Requests:** request materials for a job; you'll only see your
  own department's requests (Store and Manager also see them, to action
  them).
- **Costing:** run a quick cost estimate for a job using the current rate
  master.

## Dispatch

- **Dispatch:** create a dispatch order (recipient, quantity, courier/vehicle)
  and move it through Pending → Ready → Dispatched → Delivered as it
  progresses.

## Accounts / Budget

- **Accounts & Budgets:** record expenses and set budget lines by
  department/category/year (optionally by month). You'll get an in-app
  alert when a department crosses 80% of its budget.
- **Suppliers & Purchase Orders:** you share this view with Store for
  purchasing visibility.
- **Costing:** read-only access to the rate master for budgeting purposes.

## General Employee

- **Material Requests:** submit your own requests and track their status.
  You don't see other departments' data.

## Live updates

While you're logged in, a small indicator in the bottom-left of the sidebar
shows whether you're receiving live updates. When someone in Store issues
stock, or a request you submitted is approved, you'll see a notification
pop up automatically - no need to refresh the page.
